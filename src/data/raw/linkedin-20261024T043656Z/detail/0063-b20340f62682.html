Key Responsibilities

  • Patient Consultation & Care: Conduct general illness and wellness consultations, perform pre-procedure assessments, and provide follow-up care to ensure treatment continuity.
  • Medical Documentation & Compliance: Maintain accurate medical records and ensure compliance with clinical safety standards and regulatory requirements.
  • Clinical & Procedural Support: Assist with complex blood draws and needle insertions when required.
  • Health Education & Corporate Engagement: Deliver health talks and participate in corporate wellness initiatives and internal medical training.
  • Professional Licensing & Continuous Learning: Ensure timely renewal of Annual Practicing Certificate (APC) and actively participate in professional development activities.
  • Ad-Hoc Responsibilities: Perform additional duties as assigned by management to support smooth clinical operations and team effectiveness.


Who We Are Looking For

  • Qualifications: MBBS or equivalent medical degree recognized by MMC.
  • Registration: Fully registered with Malaysian Medical Council (MMC) with valid Annual Practicing Certificate (APC).
  • Experience: Prior experience in general practice, integrative, preventive, or wellness medicine is an advantage.
  • Languages: Proficiency in English and Bahasa Malaysia is required. Ability to communicate effectively in Chinese to engage with Chinese-speaking patients is essential.
  • Personal Attributes: Professional, ethical, patient-focused, and committed to continuous improvement.
  • Location: Johor Bahru
