Your impact

At Jacobs, our Energy & Power business is shaping the future of energy by delivering innovative, sustainable, and reliable solutions across thermal, renewable, and emerging technologies. As the energy landscape continues to evolve, our teams play a critical role in helping clients navigate complex challenges while driving meaningful progress toward a cleaner and more resilient future.

We are seeking a Senior Civil Engineer to join our Energy & Power team across APAC, supporting the delivery of high-quality engineering and consultancy services on complex infrastructure projects. This role will contribute to the successful delivery of projects through technical excellence, collaboration across multidisciplinary teams, and proactive client engagement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver high-quality civil engineering designs and consultancy services
  • Perform design, review, and technical assurance activities
  • Support project delivery to meet scope, schedule, budget, and quality targets
  • Collaborate across multidisciplinary teams to deliver integrated solutions
  • Build and maintain client relationships and support business development
  • Prepare and review design calculations, reports, and documentation
  • Ensure compliance with QA standards and processes
  • Mentor junior engineers and support team development
  • Monitor workload, utilisation, and deliverables to ensure effective time management and alignment with team priorities


Here's what you'll need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering (or equivalent)
  • Minimum 10+ years’ experience in civil engineering design and consultancy
  • Well-developed technical and leadership capability in roads, drainage, and pavement design
  • Proficiency in relevant engineering tools, including AutoCAD, Civil 3D, or equivalent civil design software
  • Experience in power sector projects is highly preferred
