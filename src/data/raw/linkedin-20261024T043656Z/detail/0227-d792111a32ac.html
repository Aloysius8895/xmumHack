About the company

We are one of the world’s earliest and most established digital asset platforms, serving tens of millions of users across global markets. Consistently ranked among the top exchanges worldwide, we support a comprehensive suite of trading and wealth management products across thousands of digital assets. With thousands of employees spanning key financial hubs globally, we operate with a remote-first, high-autonomy culture that attracts talent who thrive in fast-moving, intellectually demanding environments. We are building the financial infrastructure of the next era of the internet.


Why This Role

In on-chain trading, loyalty lasts exactly as long as your tool is the fastest route to a fresh token, a wallet worth following, or a clean fill. We’re putting on-chain trading in front of an exchange user base most standalone on-chain apps can only dream of. Get it right, and serious on-chain traders make us their main venue instead of just a place to park funds. Over the next 12 months, you’ll ship the discovery and execution flow, the Meme and social trading layer, and the data tools traders check every day, then set the direction for what comes after.


What You’ll Do

  • Own how traders find and trade new tokens: discovery feeds, limit / take-profit / stop-loss orders, and quick execution across chains
  • Ship Meme and social trading features such as copy trading, smart-money tracking, and launchpad and trending-token flows
  • Build data products that hand traders an edge: alerts and signals, wallet analysis, and rankings
  • Decide what gets built next using on-chain and user data, and measure each release on volume, activity and retention
  • Own the PRDs and take features from discovery to launch alongside engineering, design and community


What You’ll Bring

Non-negotiables

  • You’ve taken on-chain trading or Meme / social trading products live with real users
  • You’re an active on-chain trader with first-hand feel for Meme cycles, launchpad mechanics and wallet behaviour
  • You can turn on-chain data into product: you’ve designed signals, wallet analytics or ranking features, and your priorities follow the data
  • You write PRDs engineers can build straight from, and you get features out the door across teams

Bonus

  • Daily use of popular on-chain trading terminals
  • Hands-on with on-chain analytics dashboards or quant trading
  • A tool product background at a major Web2 tech company, or founder experience on a Meme project
