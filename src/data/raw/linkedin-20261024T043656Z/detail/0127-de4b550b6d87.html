Celeste Tan

Principal & Early Childhood Educator of Babies & Casa Montessori International Preschool

幼儿园华文老师


Company: Babies & Casa Montessori

Location: Kajang, Selangor

Job Type: Full-Time


Key Responsibilities
  • Plan and deliver engaging Mandarin lessons for preschool children aged 3–6.
  • Introduce Mandarin through storytelling, songs, games, creative activities and hands-on learning.
  • Prepare age-appropriate lesson plans, teaching materials and classroom activities.
  • Observe and monitor children's learning progress and development.
  • Create a positive, safe and nurturing classroom environment.
  • Work closely with fellow teachers to support children's overall development.
  • Communicate effectively with parents regarding children's learning when required.
  • Participate in school events, activities and teacher development programmes.


Requirements
  • Good command of spoken and written Mandarin.
  • Passionate about teaching and working with children.
  • Patient, loving, responsible, creative and energetic.
  • Good communication skills and a positive team-oriented attitude.
  • Previous preschool or kindergarten teaching experience is preferred.
  • Fresh graduates with a passion for early childhood education are encouraged to apply.
  • Diploma or Degree in Early Childhood Education, Education or a related field is an added advantage.
  • Familiarity with Montessori teaching approaches is an advantage, but not compulsory.


Benefits:

  • Competitive salary commensurate with experience.
  • Extra Leaves (Annual Leave, Medical Leave, School Holiday Leave, Festivities Leave, Compassionate Leave etc)
  • Medical care provided.
  • Performance bonus opportunities
  • EPF, SOCSO & EIS contributions
  • Birthday celebrations
  • Annual company trip
  • Annual company dinner
  • Emphasis on work-life balance
  • Professional development opportunities to enhance teaching skills.
  • Collaborative and supportive work environment.
  • Opportunities for career growth within our organization.


If you are enthusiastic about providing high-quality education and care for young children and meet the qualifications above, we would love to hear from you! Please submit your resume to us at career@babiescasa.com.
