At TNG Digital, part of TNG Digital Group, we build the tech behind TNG eWallet, one of Malaysia’s most used apps for payments and everyday life.


Millions of people rely on us daily, not just to pay, but to manage money, access financial services, and get things done faster and simpler.


Behind it all is a team that likes to question how things are done, move quickly, and try new ideas. If you enjoy solving real problems and seeing your work used at scale, you will feel right at home here.


What You'll Do:

  • End-to-end design process, such as prototyping, user flow, wireframing, high fidelity UI, usability testing, handoffs to developers, etc. with some guidance from seniors.
  • Work closely with senior designers to conduct user research, analyse findings and uncover insights.
  • Documentation of work and conclude a project with its success metrics.
  • Deliver in-app or web UIUX design in collaboration with designers, writers, developers, marketing and product owners.
  • Adhere to TNG eWallet Design Systems and contributing to the continuing development of Design Systems.
  • Follow up assigned projects and task timely and diligently.


Role Requirements:

  • 4+ years of product design experience.
  • Experienced in building or maintaining design systems / design language in web and mobile apps is a plus.
  • Have succeeded in projects with support of UX design principles, or psychology of design and customer profile building.
  • Demonstrate your skills, thought process and knowledge with an online portfolio.
  • Proficiency in product design tools such as Figma, Marvelapp, Lottiefiles, Zeplin, and/or Adobe Creative Suite.
  • Basic understanding in UX research process and best practices.
  • Strong layout design, colour sense and typography skills.
  • Optional to have understanding on development technicality for communication with engineers.
