ROLE OVERVIEW 

The AI & ML Engineer designs, builds, and deploys machine learning models and AI solutions that convert complex data into actionable insights. You will develop, evaluate and implement AI/ML models and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver intelligent solutions that drive business outcomes.


KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • DEVELOP AI & ML SOLUTIONS
  • Design, build and train machine learning and deep learning models for classification, regression, clustering, time series forecasting, recommendation systems and more.
  • COMPUTER VISION & VIDEO ANALYTICS
  • Develop and optimise computer vision models to detect, count and classify objects, estimate speed, queue length and detect incidents from traffic video streams with high accuracy.
  • ADVANCED ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS
  • Create dashboards, analytical reports and visualizations that reveal trends, patterns and performance indicators to support operational and strategic decisions.
  • DATA PREPARATION & MODELING
  • Transform raw data into high-quality datasets. Perform feature engineering, model selection, training, validation and hyperparameter tuning.
  • PREDICTIVE MODELING & FORECASTING
  • Build predictive models for traffic flow, demand forecasting and incident likelihood to enable proactive planning and decision-making.
  • MODEL DEPLOYMENT & MONITORING
  • Deploy AI/ML models to production environments, monitor performance, detect drift and retrain models to maintain accuracy and reliability.
  • MODEL GOVERNANCE & ETHICS
  • Ensure models are explainable, unbiased, secure and compliant with data privacy requirements and ethical AI practices.
  • COLLABORATE & INNOVATE
  • Work closely with data engineers, developers, domain experts and stakeholders to deliver innovative, data-driven solutions that create real impact.


REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering or related field.
  • Professional certifications are an advantage:
  • Machine Learning (Coursera, edX, AWS, Google, Microsoft)
  • Data Science / AI
  • TensorFlow Developer / NVIDIA DLI / Databricks / etc.
  • Strong foundation in mathematics, statistics, probability and linear algebra.


REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in AI, machine learning or data analytics.
  • At least 3 years of hands-on experience developing and deploying ML models in real-world environments.
  • Experience in computer vision and video analytics is an advantage.
  • Proven experience working with large datasets, real-time data streams and high-volume data pipelines.
  • Proficiency in Python and ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, OpenCV).
  • Experience with MLOps, model deployment, API integration and production monitoring.
  • Experience in transportation, smart city, surveillance or traffic analytics projects is highly desirable.


KEY COMPETENCIES

  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning
  • Computer Vision & Image Processing
  • Data Analytics & Visualization
  • Predictive Modeling & Forecasting
  • Statistical Analysis
  • Python Programming
  • Big Data & Data Engineering Concepts
  • MLOps & Model Deployment
  • SQL & Data Management
  • Analytical Thinking & Problem Solving
  • Communication & Collaboration
  • Innovation & Continuous Learning


TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES

  • Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, OpenCV, PostgreSQL, pandas, Apache Spark, Power BI, Docker


WHAT YOU WILL WORK ON

  • Real-time Traffic Analytics
  • Incident Detection & Alerts
  • Traffic Flow & Congestion Prediction
  • Vehicle Classification & Counting
  • Travel Time & Speed Estimation
  • Operational & Strategic Insights
