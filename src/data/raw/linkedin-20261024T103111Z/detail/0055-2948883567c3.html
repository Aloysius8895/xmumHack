As a Sales Engineer at Branch, you will:


Pre-Sales Support

  • Partner with Account Executives across SEA to understand customer goals, technical environments, and success criteria.
  • Lead product demonstrations, technical deep dives, and solution architecture discussions across Branch’s attribution, deep linking, and engagement products.
  • Design and build custom solutions, proofs of concept, and sandbox integrations that showcase Branch’s capabilities against a prospect’s specific use case.

Technical Expertise

  • Serve as the subject matter expert on Branch’s platform, APIs, SDKs, and integration options, including our Journeys, Universal Ads, and Ivy AI product lines.
  • Stay current on mobile measurement standards (SKAN, AdAttributionKit, GDPR/DPDP-style privacy regimes), ad fraud trends, and the broader mobile and marketing technology landscape.
  • Advise customers on integrating Branch into existing mobile, web, and data infrastructure, including CDPs, MMPs migrations, and analytics stacks.

Customer Engagement

  • Build trusted relationships with engineering, product, and growth marketing stakeholders, addressing technical concerns and ensuring long-term success on the platform.
  • Act as a strategic advisor to customers, recommending best practices that maximize ROI and reduce time-to-value from Branch’s solutions.

Collaboration and Feedback

  • Work closely with Product and Engineering to relay customer needs, competitive intelligence, and regional market trends from the SEA region.
  • Build and maintain technical enablement resources — documentation, demo environments, and presentations — that scale the sales engineering function across the team.

Team Structure & Reporting

  • This role will report directly to the Regional General Manager (GM), based in Singapore.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams across Sales and Customer Success to support enterprise growth across the APAC region.

Travel Expectations

  • This position will require travel to customer locations across the SEA region for customer meetings, product demonstrations, solution design sessions, and other enterprise engagements.


You’ll be a strong fit for this role if you have:

  • 5+ years of experience in Sales Engineering, Solutions Engineering, Solutions Consulting, or another customer-facing technical role, ideally in SaaS, martech, or adtech.
  • A strong understanding of APIs, SDKs, system integrations, and modern SaaS/cloud architectures.
  • Working knowledge of iOS and Android application development concepts and mobile app architectures; hands-on development experience is a plus but not required.
  • A track record of leading technical discovery, solution design, product demonstrations, and Proofs of Concept with enterprise customers.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate complex technical concepts for both engineering and business audiences.
  • Familiarity with mobile measurement, attribution, deep linking, or broader marketing technology platforms is strongly preferred.
  • A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.


Nice to Have

  • Familiarity with competing MMP/attribution platforms to support competitive displacement conversations.
  • Experience with discovery and qualification frameworks (e.g., MEDDIC) for running joint technical discovery with Account Executives.
  • Comfort presenting ROI and business-value narratives to VP- and C-level stakeholders, not only engineering counterparts.
  • Experience responding to RFPs and security questionnaires, particularly in regulated industries such as fintech.
  • Experience navigating multi-stakeholder, matrixed sales cycles spanning engineering, marketing, and data teams.
  • AI/LLM literacy, with the ability to speak to how AI-driven recommendations and agents work, including their data and privacy implications — relevant to Branch’s Ivy product.
  • Familiarity with retail media and first-party data strategy, useful when selling into retail and e-commerce accounts.


Why Join Branch

  • Join a category-defining team building the AI-powered future of mobile measurement, attribution, and deep linking.
  • Play a pivotal role in the success of Branch’s customers across the fast-growing APAC market.
  • Competitive compensation and a comprehensive benefits package.
  • A collaborative, inclusive culture with real investment in your professional growth.
  • Ongoing opportunities for learning, mentorship, and career development.
