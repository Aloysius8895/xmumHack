Responsibilities:

  • Conduct thorough assessments through in person to formulate an appropriate diagnosis and treatment plans.
  • Request relevant investigations and interpret the results obtained.
  • Prescribe medications and treatments as per established protocols.
  • Develop and implement patient management plans while collaborating with multidisciplinary teams where required.
  • Document patient interactions and maintain accurate medical records.
  • Provide medical consultations and perform clinical procedures in accordance with established clinical guidelines, medical protocols and professional standards.
  • Maintain professional standards of clinical knowledge, communication and behaviour with patients and colleagues.
  • Engage in continuing education and training opportunities to enhance medical expertise.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues to perform related medical duties as per service requirements.


Requirements:

  • Graduated with a Bachelor Degree in Medicine and Surgery (MBBS) or an equivalent medical degree.
  • At least 5 years experience in Medical field
  • Registered with the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC).
  • Holds a valid and updated Annual Practicing Certificate (APC).
  • Gained at least five(5) years of experience working in a related field.
  • Required to work 45 hours per week on a shift basis, including Saturday and Sunday.
