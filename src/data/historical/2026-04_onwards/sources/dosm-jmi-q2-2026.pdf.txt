BIG DATA ANALYTICS: JOB MARKET INSIGHTS, SECOND QUARTER OF 2026

Job  Market  Insights are  quarterly  releases  based  on  the  job  oﬀerings  advertised  online
through major private recruitment platforms. This report contains key information on job
oﬀerings by major  occupational groups and economic activities.
 
Job Oﬀering Advertised Online, Second Quarter of 2026
The number of online job oﬀerings advertised in the second quarter of 2026 decreased by
11.4 per cent to 555,509.
 
Job Oﬀering Advertised Online for Major Group Occupation
Online job oﬀerings were concentrated in the Professional category, accounting for 51.5 per
cent  or  285,907  job  oﬀerings  in  the  second  quarter  of  2026,  compared  with  315,768  job
oﬀerings  recorded  in  the  ﬁrst  quarter  of  2026.  Managers  category  recorded  87,950  job
oﬀerings,  followed  by  Technicians  and  Associate  Professionals  with  86,512  job  oﬀerings.
Higher demand was observed for Advertising and Marketing Professionals (40,640 job
oﬀerings),  followed  by  Managing  Directors  and  Chief  Executives  (26,649  job  oﬀerings),
Administrative  Associate  Professionals  (23,984  job  oﬀerings),  Accountants  and  Auditors
(22,067  job  oﬀerings)  and  Software  Developers  (19,899  job  oﬀerings).
 
Job Oﬀering Advertised Online for Economic Activities
The Services sector continued to dominate, accounting for 43.9 per cent or 243,896 job
oﬀerings in the second quarter of 2026, down from 78.8 per cent in the ﬁrst quarter of 2026.
The Wholesale and Retail Trade; Repair of Motor Vehicles and Motorcycles subsector
recorded  the  highest  number  of  job  oﬀerings  within  the  Services  sector,  at  78,526  job
oﬀerings, followed by Professional, Scientiﬁc and Technical Activities (32,127 job oﬀerings),
Administrative  and  Support  Service  Activities  (28,788  job  oﬀerings),  Accommodation  and
Food Service Activities (20,629 job oﬀerings), and Financial and Insurance/Takaful Activities
(20,593 job oﬀerings).
