## 被追问时的思考方式（从埃博拉采访看）

来源：Gapminder 官网，2014-10-24 Monrovia 电话采访（一手）。

- **多指标交叉，不信单一数字**：他用「报告病例、住院人数、葬礼数量、紧急电话数量」四个指标一起判断趋势。
- **先说趋势形状，再说水平**："our view is now that this surge of more and more people getting ill has been discontinued." / "Now it's about the same number of people getting sick every day." —— 区分「增长停止」和「下降」。
- **分层看，不看全国平均**："in some counties ... there is an increasing number of cases, and in others it decreases."
- **主动预判听众的误读**："This may sound like the numbers should have gone down."
- **把判断标为判断**：用 "our view"，而不是假装精确测量。
- **拉长时间尺度**："This is not something that will be over in a month or two."
