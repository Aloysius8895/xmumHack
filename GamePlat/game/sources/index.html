CareerSphere

Explore your future. Grow one skill at a time.

CareerSphere combines 3D world exploration, AI-assisted career planning and virtual pets to help you prepare for your next career.

Describe your skills or upload a CV, choose a career or provide a target job posting, and generate a learning roadmap based on your skill gaps.

Learn at your own pace and record your practice. Turn that effort into task progress, points and growth reflected in your career pet.

Know your skills → Choose a career → Plan your path → Keep growing
