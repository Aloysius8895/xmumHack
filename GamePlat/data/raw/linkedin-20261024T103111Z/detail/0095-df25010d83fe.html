Are you a hands-on Data Engineering Architect who can confidently discuss enterprise architecture with CTOs and still dive deep into Spark tuning when needed?

We're building a fast-growing Databricks Practice delivering enterprise-scale Data, Analytics, and AI solutions across APAC, and we're looking for a Lead Solution Architect to help shape the future of our practice.

Language Skills: Professional proficiency in Mandarin and/or Bahasa Malaysia is required to effectively support stakeholder communication and client engagement.

Architecture & Delivery

  • Lead end-to-end architecture and solution design for Databricks implementations.
  • Build scalable, secure, and high-performance Lakehouse platforms.
  • Deliver enterprise-grade data solutions covering ingestion, transformation, governance, orchestration, and analytics.
  • Drive large-scale cloud migrations from legacy Data Warehouses, Hadoop, and on-prem platforms to Databricks on Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  • Provide hands-on leadership through code reviews, performance tuning, debugging, and best-practice implementation.
  • Establish engineering standards and reusable frameworks for the practice.

Presales & Client Engagement

  • Lead discovery workshops, solution assessments, PoCs, RFP responses, and proposal development.
  • Present and defend solution architectures with CIOs, CTOs, and Architecture Review Boards.
  • Translate complex technical concepts into clear business outcomes for executive stakeholders.
  • Support effort estimation, pricing strategies, and project planning for successful deal conversion.

Practice Building

  • Mentor and lead Data Engineers and Architects.
  • Create accelerators, reusable assets, and frameworks to improve delivery efficiency.
  • Convert successful client engagements into case studies and go-to-market offerings.
  • Contribute to the growth and strategic direction of the Databricks practice.

What We're Looking For

✅ 12+ years of experience in Data Engineering, Data Architecture, or Cloud Data Platforms

✅ 3+ years of enterprise-scale Databricks experience

✅ Strong expertise in Apache Spark, performance tuning, streaming, and troubleshooting

✅ Deep knowledge of Databricks Lakehouse, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, Medallion Architecture, and governance frameworks

✅ Experience with Databricks Workflows, Delta Live Tables (DLT), Lakeflow, Airflow, or Azure Data Factory

✅ Proven track record leading large-scale cloud migration and modernization programs

✅ Strong hands-on skills in Python, SQL, and Scala

✅ Experience with CI/CD and DevOps for Data Platforms

✅ Familiarity with MLflow, AI/GenAI integrations, and LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic)

✅ Strong stakeholder management and presales experience

✅ Databricks Professional Certifications highly preferred

Why Join Us?

  • Work directly with enterprise clients on large-scale transformation programs.
  • Influence solution strategy, delivery excellence, and business growth.
