About the Group

Dyna (Dynasynergy Technology Sdn Bhd) was founded in 2002 and provides off-grid, solar-hybrid and grid-connected power solutions to telecommunications companies, state utilities and large industrials across Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak. Anchor customers include SESB, U Mobile, Maxis, Sarawak Digital Economy Corporation and the Royal Malaysian Navy. Certifications include Suruhanjaya Tenaga Class A, CIDB G7 and ISO 9001:2015.

Rooftop Energy, founded in 2025, is the solar arm of Altus Energy, the wider group that Dyna is also part of. It delivers commercial and industrial solar Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) projects and builds RooftopIQ, a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform where solar EPC teams run their projects and monitor installed systems.

The combined group has more than 70 staff across Shah Alam (HQ) and Kota Kinabalu.


About the Role

Rooftop Energy is hiring a full-stack engineer to build the go-to-market (GTM) tools of its solar EPC team. Internally the seat is called GTM Engineer.

The EPC team's Project Engineers design, sell and commission commercial and industrial rooftop solar systems. Most new projects reach them today through referrals. This role builds and runs the team's GTM tooling so they can find, qualify and win more of that work themselves: prospecting and lead screening, outreach automation, proposal automation and pipeline tracking. You write the code, run it on live deals with the Project Engineers and keep what wins work.


What You Will Do

  • Own the EPC team's GTM tooling. Build, run and maintain the tools that take a lead from first contact to a signed proposal, and decide with the Chief Engineer what to build next.
  • Prospecting and lead screening. Build prospect lists of commercial and industrial buildings across Malaysia from public data, company registries, industrial park listings and satellite imagery. Rank them with a lead-screening tool that scores roof size, building type, tariff category and indicative system size, so the Project Engineers spend their time on the sites most likely to proceed.
  • Outreach automation. Build and run automated email and LinkedIn sequences. Find and enrich decision makers' contact details, schedule follow-ups and log every reply and meeting without anyone copying it by hand. Test messaging and measure what gets replies.
  • Proposal automation. Build on the team's proposal tools: the automated bill of quantities and financial model, the roof-plan take-off that reads panel count, roof area and cable routes off a layout drawing, and the client proposal deck. Join them up so a site's inputs go in once and a checked proposal comes out.
  • Pipeline automation and reporting. Connect the tools to the EPC Master Tracker so every lead has a source, a stage, an owner and an estimated value, and report on what converts by channel.
  • Work alongside the Project Engineers every week. Sit in on site visits and customer meetings when useful, hear what slows a deal down, and turn it into the next tool or fix.
