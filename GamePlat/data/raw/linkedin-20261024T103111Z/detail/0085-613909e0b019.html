Role: AI Project Engineer


Job Responsibilities

  • Develop and enhance AI/Generative AI applications using Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Design and implement RAG pipelines for intelligent knowledge retrieval and question-answering solutions.
  • Build AI agents and agentic workflows for reasoning, planning, tool usage, and task automation.
  • Develop and integrate MCP tools to connect AI applications with enterprise systems, databases, APIs, and engineering services.
  • Develop end-to-end AI solutions from PoC and prototyping through implementation, testing, deployment, and support.
  • Integrate AI capabilities with APIs, databases, backend services, and engineering applications.
  • Work with prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, and semantic search to improve AI solution quality.
  • Evaluate, test, debug, and optimize AI solutions for accuracy, reliability, performance, and scalability.
  • Apply good software engineering practices including version control, testing, documentation, and CI/CD.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business and engineering requirements into practical AI solutions.


Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • At least 2-6 years of related working experiences.
  • Hands-on experience developing AI/Generative AI solutions, such as LLM applications, RAG systems, AI agents, or AI-driven automation.
  • Strong programming skills in Python.
  • Good understanding of LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, prompt engineering, embeddings, and vector databases.
  • Experience with REST APIs, backend development, databases, and system integration.
  • Familiarity with SQL, Git/GitHub, testing, debugging, and software development lifecycle practices.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills.


Preferred Skills

  • Experience with Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or open-source LLMs.
  • Experience with LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, Semantic Kernel, or similar Agentic AI frameworks.
  • Understanding of MCP (Model Context Protocol) and tool integrations.
  • Experience with FastAPI, Docker/containers, cloud platforms, DevOps, or CI/CD.
  • Exposure to PyTorch, TensorFlow, LLMOps, AI evaluation, model benchmarking, or AI application monitoring.
  • Experience taking AI solutions from PoC/prototype to deployment.
