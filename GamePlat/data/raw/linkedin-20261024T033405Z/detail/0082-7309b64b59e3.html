About Respond.io

Founded in Hong Kong in early 2017, respond.io is an AI-powered business messaging platform that helps companies manage customer conversations across chat, calls and email — all in one place.


Trusted by businesses in over 127 countries and recognized by G2 and SME100, respond.io enables fast-growing companies around the world to capture, convert, and retain customers at scale.


We operate as a globally distributed team with employees based around the world, contributing to a diverse and inclusive culture. Join us, and be part of a team that is shaping the future of customer conversation management!

Role Description

You'll join our Frontend Department as a Senior Frontend Developer, taking ownership of complex modules within our Vue 3 single-page application. You'll own code development, automation, and testing to ship functional, secure, and user-friendly features, working closely with our Backend Department, raising the bar on code quality, and mentoring mid-level and junior developers.


What You Will Be Doing

  • Influence architecture decisions and technical direction of different modules, not just implementation.
  • Shape the structure and design of platform modules and REST APIs, including real-time features on Web Sockets.
  • Own complex product features end-to-end, working closely with the Backend Department.
  • Set up and maintain CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions) to automate builds and testing.
  • Utilized AI coding tools (e.g. Claude Code/Cursor) and AI-assisted testing/review as a default workflow, not a fallback.
  • Balance functional and aesthetic design, keeping every screen responsive and on-brand across devices.
  • Develop reusable, well-documented frontend components and libraries the wider team can build on.
  • Optimize application speed and scalability as our user base and feature set grow.
  • Review code for quality, consistency, and performance, sharing feedback that helps the team improve.
  • Troubleshoot and debug frontend issues quickly to keep the user experience smooth.
  • Coach and mentor mid-level and junior developers as they grow into the team.


Qualifications

  • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 3-4+ years of relevant software development and/or testing experience.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Vue.js, ideally Vue 3.
  • Firm grasp of JavaScript (ES6+), Typescript, HTML5, and Tailwind CSS.
  • Experience building Single Page Applications (SPA) using Web Sockets.
  • Proficient with Vite/Rollup build tooling and Git/GitHub, including CI/CD (GitHub Actions).
  • Vitest and Vue Test Utils for unit testing, Playwright for e2e testing.
  • Self-starter, comfortable working independently under pressure.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, able to isolate tricky bugs quickly.
  • Clear communicator in English, comfortable explaining complex ideas verbally and in writing.
  • Node.js, Tan Stack Query, and Design Systems experience (with Storybook) is a plus.
  • Performance profiling, bundle optimization, PWA, i18n, and Sentry familiarity is a plus.
  • Exposure to a UI library such as Vuetify, Quasar, Shadcn, or Reka UI is a plus.
  • GitHub profile link in your application is a plus.
