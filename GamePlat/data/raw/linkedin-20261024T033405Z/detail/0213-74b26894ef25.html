At Nadi Tech, part of TNG Digital Group, we build and run secure, high-performance digital infrastructure that powers essential services across Malaysia.


Our platforms support large-scale B2B and public sector initiatives used by millions of people every day. That means reliability, security, and performance are not nice-to-haves. They are built into everything we do from day one.


We are looking for people who enjoy solving complex, real-world problems. People who think in systems, understand how things connect, and take pride in building infrastructure that is stable, scalable, and built to last.


Here, you will take ownership of systems that matter and see your work used in real, everyday scenarios.


What You'll Do:

  • Translate business requests and prototypes into high quality applications.
  • Apply software development practices including E2E SDLC, understanding business specifications, design, coding, test, deployment, release, monitor, support, etc.
  • Ensure good coding practices and maintain high coding standard.
  • Continuously research, evaluate and implement new mobile technologies and features.
  • Work on bug fixing and improving application performance.
  • Maintain Web Distribution Channel, such as Web Servers, CDN and more.
  • Able to provide suggestion to the team to improve team’s capabilities, application quality and development process. Automation knowledge is a bonus.
  • Able to independently drive projects to meet business goals.


Role Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Development in HTML5, CSS3, JS ES6 language. (Bonus - Typescript, LESS and SASS).
  • Solid understanding of web distribution channels, such as Web Servers, CDN and more.
  • Experience with Angular, Vue, React
  • Knowledge in transpiler tools, build tools, CICD methodology and application security
  • Intermediate experience with REST, JSON and JWT.
  • Intermediate Experience with third party library and APIs
  • Intermediate Experience with Unit Test. (Bonus - TDD, BDD and Automation)
  • Intermediate Experience with software design pattern
