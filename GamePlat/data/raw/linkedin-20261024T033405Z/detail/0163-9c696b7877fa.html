Job Description

What You'll Do :

  • Design with Purpose: Create flows, wireframes, prototypes, and polished designs that improve discoverability, conversion, and trust.
  • Collaborate Cross-Functionally: Partner with Product Managers, Engineers, and Category Leads to align design solutions with product strategy.
  • Embed User Insights: Use research, feedback, and analytics to inform and validate design decisions.
  • Contribute to Design Systems: Strengthen Mudah’s design system to ensure consistency and scalability across products.
  • Iterate & Improve: Rapidly test, refine, and optimize designs based on user needs and performance data.

Qualifications

  • Experience: 3-4 years or above in product/UX/UI design, with a proven ability to deliver user-facing products and actively integrate generative AI tools into daily design workflows.
  • Portfolio of Outcomes: Work that demonstrates problem-solving, design craft, and measurable product or user impact.
  • User-Centric & Analytical: Ability to balance qualitative insights with quantitative data in making design decisions.
  • Collaboration Skills: Strong communicator who thrives in cross-functional environments.
  • Design Craft & Tools: Proficiency in Figma or equivalent, with experience contributing to design systems.
