Job Description

  • Lead end-to-end UX/UI projects from problem discovery to final design delivery.
  • Use AI-powered tools to accelerate research, ideation, user flows, wireframing, prototyping, and design exploration.
  • Explore and validate multiple design solutions using AI-assisted workflows.
  • Develop user journeys, information architecture, and intuitive interaction flows.
  • Translate complex business requirements and user needs into clear, scalable digital experiences.
  • Design high-quality interfaces for web, mobile, enterprise systems, and digital products.
  • Work with Figma, design systems, and component libraries to ensure design consistency and efficiency.
  • Collaborate with Product, Technology, AI, and Engineering teams to ensure designs are practical and user-focused.
  • Present design concepts and decisions clearly to clients, stakeholders, and internal teams.
  • Use prototypes and AI-assisted simulations to validate ideas before development.
  • Identify opportunities to improve usability, accessibility, efficiency, and overall user experience.
  • Stay updated on emerging AI tools, technologies, and design workflows to continuously improve the design process.



What We’re Looking For

  • 5+ years of professional UI/UX design experience with a strong portfolio.
  • Strong understanding of user-centred design, UX, UI, usability, and design systems.
  • Proficient in Figma and experienced with design systems and component libraries.
  • Experience designing for web, mobile, and enterprise applications.
  • Able to translate complex requirements into clear and intuitive user experiences.
  • Comfortable using or learning AI tools for research, ideation, prototyping, and design iteration.
  • Able to critically evaluate AI-generated ideas using strong design judgement.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and comfortable working with ambiguity.
  • Able to balance user needs, business objectives, and technical requirements.
  • Takes ownership of projects from concept through final delivery.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain design decisions clearly.
  • Able to collaborate effectively with clients, developers, product teams, and other stakeholders.
  • Open to feedback and continuous improvement.
  • Experience mentoring or supporting junior designers is a plus.
