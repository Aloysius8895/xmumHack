Role Overview

A versatile professional combining strong design execution with deep user research expertise. Capable of leading UX/UI design for Crypto trading products, while leveraging qualitative research to drive design decisions — covering the full loop, from identifying problems to delivering solutions.


Key Responsibilities

  1. Product Design: Lead UX/UI design for Crypto trading products across Web and mobile platforms, ensuring high-quality delivery.
  2. User Research: Plan and conduct user interviews, usability testing, and surveys to uncover user behaviors and needs.
  3. Insight-Driven Decision Making: Translate research findings into actionable design solutions and product recommendations that shape product direction.
  4. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Product, Engineering, and Business teams to balance business objectives with user experience goals.


Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Design, HCI, or UX-related fields; overseas study experience preferred.
  • 3–5 years of UI/UX design or user research experience with end-to-end project involvement.
  • Excellent bilingual communication skills (Chinese & English), able to distill complex findings into clear, actionable recommendations.
  • Proficient in Figma, AI, and mainstream research & analytics tools.
  • Strong product thinking and interaction design mindset; able to understand business context and independently produce design solutions.
  • Passion for or experience in financial trading, cryptocurrency, blockchain, or Web3 preferred.
  • Experience in growth design or AI-related design is a plus.


Nice to Have

  • Research or design experience with Crypto/trading products
  • Portfolio demonstrating how research insights drove design outcomes
