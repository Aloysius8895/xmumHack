As a Staff Auditor here, you’ll be part of our Regional Audit team located in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia. You’ll get to:


The Job

  • Perform audit procedures, including walkthroughs, control testing, and substantive analytics.
  • Support inventory activities, including inventory counts for external auditors under the Company Direct Assistance Program.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support compliance and internal audits.
  • Develop and implement audit plans, monitor progress, and ensure adherence to company policies and procedures.
  • Document audit work, findings, and recommendations clearly and accurately.
  • Identify control gaps and process improvement opportunities.
  • Support special projects and ad hoc assignments, as needed.


Key Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
  • Minimum 2 years of audit experience with a Big 4 firm or a public company.
  • Manufacturing industry experience preferred.
  • Strong knowledge of US GAAP and PCAOB requirements.
  • Solid understanding of internal control frameworks and auditing standards.
  • Professional certifications such as CPA, CMA, or CIA are preferred.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills.
  • Effective interpersonal, verbal, and written communication skills in English. Mandarin Chinese is a plus.
  • Ability to prioritize tasks, manage multiple assignments, and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.


Additional Information

  • Opportunity to add value to the business and impact the bottom line.
  • Work alongside a diverse, highly skilled team with full support from senior management and the Audit Committee.
  • Primarily remote work arrangement.
  • Domestic and international travel required (approximately 30%).
  • The role requires regular movement across large production and distribution areas.
