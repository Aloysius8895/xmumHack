Company Overview:

DK Bank is a leading Bhutanese financial institution serving as the official bank of Gelephu Mindfulness City (GMC). With a mandate to integrate innovation, sustainability, and customer-centric services with a vision to expand globally; DK Bank is pioneering new financial models for the digital era.

As we expand beyond Bhutan, we are building our Center of Excellence in Kuala Lumpur to support the next stage of DK’s growth across digital banking and financial services.

If you are looking for a role with real ownership, international exposure, and the opportunity to grow with a fast-expanding financial institution, we’d like to hear from you.


Role Purpose

You will own how DK Bank’s payment capabilities are exposed to partners, platforms, and corporate clients through APIs and integrations. You will work across Engineering, Compliance, Operations, Finance and external partners to launch and scale embedded payment products.


Key responsibilities

  1. Own the product roadmap for embedded finance offerings, including API-based payments, virtual accounts, and partner integrations.
  2. Define requirements for payment and FX flows, including incoming and outgoing transfers, cross-border (SWIFT and local rails), and other embedded finance use cases such as virtual accounts, payment collection, and partner-initiated payments.
  3. Work with partners on integration design, API specifications, sandbox testing, and go-live readiness.
  4. Coordinate UAT, defect triage, and production release with Engineering, QA, PMO, and Operations.
  5. Partner with Compliance and Risk on AML, sanctions screening, and regulatory requirements.
  6. Monitor product performance, such as transaction volumes, success rates, and partner adoption, and prioritize improvements.
  7. Develop and maintain documentation, such as user guides, API integration guides, and partner onboarding materials, alongside product specs.


Key Qualifications

  1. 3+ years of product management experience in banking, payments, or fintech.
  2. Working knowledge of payment rails and messaging (SWIFT MT/ISO 20022, local clearing) and FX concepts.
  3. Experience with APIs and testing tools such as Postman.
  4. Ability to translate business and regulatory needs into precise functional requirements.
  5. Strong cross-functional communication, including with external banks and partners.
