Role Description:


This is a full-time role for a Resident Medical Officer / Doctor based in Temerloh, Pahang. The Resident Medical Officer will provide comprehensive medical care, manage daily outpatient care, perform clinical assessments, and support medical procedures. Additional responsibilities include coordinating with other healthcare professionals, maintaining patient records, and participating in training programs to ensure high standards of healthcare delivery.


Qualifications and Further Information:


  • Minimum Medical Degree such as MD / MBBS
  • Proficiency in clinical and diagnostic management with hands-on experience in assisting or performing medical procedures
  • Ability to work collaboratively with other healthcare professionals
  • Completed compulsory service with the Ministry of Health Malaysia
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • OHD qualification is desirable but not compulsory
  • Registration with the relevant medical council and holds a valid practicing license
