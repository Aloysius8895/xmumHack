Senior UI/UX Designer

Specialisation: IT

Location: Kuala Lumpur

Let's talk business

  • You will be part of a larger global team working closely with Product Management, Software Product Owners, Engineering, QA, and other designers.
  • In this role, you'll be designing intuitive and consistent experiences for our platform, transforming complex industrial processes and data into clear, usable interfaces.
  • You'll be hands-on throughout the design process, from user research and worklflow definition to prototyping, validation, and implementation support.
  • You'll help shape and maintain our design system, ensuring consistency and scalability across products and development teams.
  • You'll own the user experience within the team, ensuring that design decisions balance user needs, business objectives, technical feasibility, and product standards.
  • Your work will directly influence how operators, supervisors, and managers interact with complex manufacturing systems across different industries and regions.

Let's talk you

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the end-to-end design of new features, enhancements, and complex enterprise workflows.
  • Translate user needs and business requirements into user journeys, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs.
  • Simplify complex manufacturing processes, dashboards, configuration screens, and data-heavy interfaces.
  • Plan and conduct user research, usability testing, and design validation.
  • Build and maintain reusable components, patterns, and guidelines within the design system.
  • Work closely with Software PO, engineers, and QA to define requirements, interactions, and acceptance criteria.
  • Support developers during implementation and perform design quality reviews.
  • Ensure consistency, usability, responsiveness, and accessibility across the platform.
  • Contribute to product and design strategy, not only design execution.
  • Promote UI/UX standards, design best practices, and continuous improvement across teams.
  • Act as a design champion, raising design maturity through hands-on contribution and collaboration.
  • Support and mentor other designers through feedback and design review.

Qualifications

  • 7+ years of experience in UI/UX, Product Design, Interaction Design, or a related field.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design experience for complex web applications or enterprise platforms.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Figma, including components, variants, auto layout, and prototyping.
  • Experience designing complex workflows, dashboards, configuration interfaces, and data visualizations.
  • Solid understanding of user-centered design, interaction design, information architecture, and usability principles.
  • Experience building or maintaining scalable design systems.
  • Ability to translate complex business and technical requirements into clear and intuitive user experiences.
  • Experience working closely with frontend engineers in an agile development environment.
  • Familiarity with Angular, Storybook, or component-based frontend development is an advantage.
  • Experience conducting user research, usability testing, and stakeholder workshops.
  • Good understanding of responsive design and accessibility standards.
  • Experience in MES, industrial, manufacturing, or enterprise platforms is a strong plus.
  • Strong analytical thinking and structured problem-solving mindset.
  • Comfortable working in international, cross-functional agile teams.
  • Able to communicate and explain design decisions clearly.
  • Fluent in English. Additional languages are a plus.
  • Self-driven, proactive, collaborative, and user-focused.
  • Agile mindset with a strong bias toward continuous improvement.

Let's make an offer you can't refuse

  • An international and innovative environment with a high-tech product range.
  • An open working atmosphere with a flat organizational structure, helpful colleagues, accessible managers, and direct access to decision-makers.
  • Close collaboration with international teams, offering global exposure and diverse perspectives.
  • Internal and external training opportunities. We’ve got your back!
  • You are ambitious and would like to grow in your role? We offer you actual advancement and development opportunities.
  • Autonomy and room for initiative. We love passionate people filled with energy and curiosity.
  • Flexible working hours aligned with core collaboration windows for effective global teamwork, with hybrid working arrangements.
  • A culture that values ownership, continuous improvement, and sustainable pace.
  • Friendly, accessible office locations with public transport convenience.
  • And of course a competitive salary with lots of fringe benefits (insurance, allowances, team building, snacks and good coffee 😊).
