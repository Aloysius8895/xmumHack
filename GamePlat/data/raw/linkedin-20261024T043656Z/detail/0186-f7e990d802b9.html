Job Summary

We are currently looking for a UX/UI designer to join our digital team. We’re looking for someone who is passionate about creating effective, intuitive and beautiful experiences.

The UX/UI Web designer will work with clients, Project Managers and technology teams to conceptualize and design our website, mobile site and eCommerce solutions we create across a range of industries.


Position Responsibilities

  • Understand user & client needs and work both independently and with our team of UX and UI designers to develop concepts and solutions.
  • Create prototypes and animation examples to fully explain experiences
  • Present sitemap, user journeys, wireframes, flows, design concepts and UI designs
  • Partner with our project managers, developers, and the members of the creative team to create consistency in the user experience on the platforms we create.
  • Work quickly and effectively in a fast-paced environment


Required Knowledge & Skills:

  • Highly developed design skills, demonstrated in your portfolio of recent work
  • Solid understanding of designing for functionality, navigation, interactions, and interfaces
  • Experience in designing systems and understanding of user journey
  • Reliable and self-motivated
  • Understanding of what is feasible with HTML/CSS/JS
  • A team player who enjoys a collaborative environment. You’re flexible and open-minded
  • Fluency in English is a must
  • Proficiency in Figma is a strong advantage
  • Experience with digital agency environment is an advantage


Prior Experience:

  • 3 - 5 years’ experience in interface design for digital applications, including websites, mobile platforms and eCommerce sites
