Job Description:

 The Legal & Compliance Counsel will be part of the APAC L&C team providing legal and compliance support for all APAC Airbus entities, in particular, Malaysia.

We are seeking a motivated and experienced In-house generalist legal counsel or lawyer with at least 6-8 years Post-Qualified Experience to join our dynamic legal team. The successful candidate will play a crucial role in providing comprehensive legal advice and support across a wide range of legal matters, including contact review, drafting, and negotiation, as well as litigation, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations, and effectively managing legal risks for the organization.

Business-level proficiency in English and Bahasa Melayu. Additional proficiency in any other APAC or European language.

More precisely:

Legal & Corporate Secretarial Matters

  • Providing legal and compliance services to the Business Units, Functions Group entities present in APAC, in particular Malaysia and their respective operations
  • Work with business stakeholders to assess legal implications and potential risk, help them identify, understand and manage both short-term and long-term risks to the business
  • Providing counsel regarding both legal framework and what is appropriate for all Airbus APAC activities across divisions
  • Provide legal support for the various acquisition, re-organisation, integration, development and divestment projects in APAC
  • Support the coordination of input of outside counsel with the internal members of the project team e.g. Group legal and compliance departments, tax, finance, HR, export control, insurance, real estate and relevant business stakeholders
  • Monitoring compliance of the transactions with relevant and applicable laws and regulations
  • Coordinate with and support where necessary other members of the legal and compliance department
  • Coordinate and review corporate secretarial documentation in respect of Airbus entities
  • Drafting and reviewing agreements for various transactions, notably:

(i) Aviation services: MRO services: parts repair, lease and exchange services, line and base airframe maintenance, spare parts management and supply, sales, fleet technical management services, inventory technical management services etc

(ii) Procurement: aircraft spare parts, aviation subcontracting services, jigs and tools, industrial maintenance, infrastructure and construction operations, general procurement

(iii) Real Estate: structure, draft and negotiate contracts relating to real estate operations (purchasing, sale, construction, lease, etc.)

(iv) Compliance-related contracts with third parties - e.g. sponsorships, resellers, channel partners, sales intermediaries

  • Drafting, adapting and managing standard contract templates depending on the business needs and ensure proper coordination with all relevant functions (Export Control, Insurance, Tax and Finance etc.)

Compliance

Under the supervision of the Regional Ethics and Compliance Manager and HO Legal & Compliance - Malaysia:

  • Support compliance initiatives and assist with adapted deployment and monitoring with the tools and systems used by Airbus
  • Support coordination within the APAC Region on policies, regulations and legal processes
  • Support the Legal & Compliance function with the roll-out and management of training sessions and clear communications on anti-corruption laws and internal compliance policies for Airbus employees based in APAC
  • Support deployment of key elements of the anti-corruption programme for due diligence, monitoring and auditing of business partners, agents, political advisors, offset providers and other types of third parties that present an elevated risk from a compliance perspective
  • Conducting of risk-based training of business partners and other third parties on Ethics & Compliance

Other duties reasonably determined from time to time by the Legal & Compliance Department and the Company

  • Assisting in monitoring all corporate matters
  • Assist with preparation and conducting of legal trainings in order to develop legal awareness for operations in APAC, in particular Malaysia
  • Supporting on coordination with external counsel on advice and support, and support internal communication/preparation on litigation/arbitration matters
  • Assist with the conducting of Compliance Investigations affecting Airbus entities (pre-investigation planning, data collection, witness interviews, final report drafting, etc.)
  • Support intake and processing of reports received through Airbus’ whistle-blowing system, “OpenLine”

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.
